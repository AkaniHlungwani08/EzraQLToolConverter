Conversion notes

  
  • Notes and assumptions made during conversion will show up here.






  Current scope: single SELECT statement, no subqueries · operators =, !=, <>, >, <, >=, <= (mapped to Eq/Neq/Gt/Lt/Gte/Lte) ·
  WHERE/ON support flat AND-only or OR-only lists (no nested "A AND (B OR C)" groups) ·
  SUM/AVG/COUNT detected as native aggregates when wrapping a single column, everything else (MIN/MAX, expressions) becomes a CUSTOM aggregate with the raw formula passed through ·
  GroupsBy is inferred from non-aggregate SELECT columns when GROUP BY is omitted · LIMIT n [OFFSET m] maps to PageSize/CurrentPage.
  Tell me if any of these assumptions are wrong and I'll adjust the mapping rules.
